## 1. Abstention as a trading decision

The idea that a predictive model should be allowed to decline to act, and that declining maps naturally onto "hold no position", is not new in finance. Pidan and El-Yaniv [`pidan2011selective`]〔仅读摘要〕 studied selective prediction of short-term financial trends with hidden Markov models, comparing a Chow-style ambiguity rejection rule with abstention in low-quality hidden states. Chalkidis and Savani [`chalkidis2021trading`] turned binary and ternary selective classifiers into trading strategies that stay flat whenever the classifier abstains. They build the selection function with the Selection-with-Guaranteed-Risk procedure of Geifman and El-Yaniv [`geifman2017selective`]〔仅读摘要〕, evaluate four model families on 30-minute bars of five CME metal futures with an anchored walk-forward scheme, and report that abstention raises accuracy on the traded subset in every configuration and improves risk-adjusted performance as slippage increases, although most configurations are unprofitable at 0.3 ticks of slippage. Nabar and Shroff [`nabar2023conservative`] train a cascade of models, each fitted on the data its predecessors were uncertain about, and take no trade when the cascade refuses to predict; their experiments use five-minute candles from the Indian equity market.

More recent work moves the abstention decision on top of an existing signal. Sanderink [`sanderink2026alpha`] separates two decisions for a LightGBM cross-sectional equity ranker: a strategy-level "regime-trust" gate, built mainly from the trailing realized rank information coefficient of matured predictions, that decides whether to trade at all on a given date, and a position-level cap that reduces exposure for the stocks with the highest predicted rank displacement. On a universe of up to 100 AI-themed U.S. stocks, the paper reports that the gate predicts model failure far better than volatility-based regime proxies and that binary abstention outperforms continuous down-weighting; it provides no formal guarantee. Fang and Ślepaczuk [`fang2026volatility`]〔仅读摘要〕 report, for the CSI 300 index, that naive predictive strategies fail after costs whereas designs with low-volatility gating and threshold calibration improve defensive performance. Selective prediction has also been applied to fixed-odds cryptocurrency contracts [`hao2026coverage`]〔仅读摘要〕, to limit-order-book forecasting [`manoharan2026uqlob`]〔仅读摘要〕, and to LLM-based forecasting pipelines [`torres2026finabstain`]〔仅读摘要；该摘要自述结果为模拟而非实证〕. At the strategy level, Guo [`guo2026whennot`]〔仅读摘要（Crossref 存入的摘要）；全文未读；作者名待核对，见 `lead_verification.md`〕 studies when a machine-learning strategy for factor and sector rotation should refuse to trade: the signal is deployed only when its validation improvement over a fallback benchmark exceeds a calibrated threshold, and otherwise the strategy abstains and holds the benchmark portfolio; the abstract also states a validation-error deployment bound and null-calibrated gates that control false deployment under placebo signals.

In quantitative-finance practice the same architecture is known as meta-labeling [`lopezdeprado2018advances`]〔仅题录；正文未读，"meta-labeling 出自该书"是外部模型给的线索加通行说法，定稿前需翻书确认章节〕: a secondary model sits on top of a primary strategy to filter false-positive signals and size positions. Joubert [`joubert2022metalabeling`]〔仅读摘要〕 consolidates the technique into a framework and decomposes it into components; Meyer, Barziy and Joubert [`meyer2023metalabeling`]〔仅读摘要〕 study calibration of the secondary model's probabilities for position sizing.

Our abstention layer is, structurally, an instance of this line of work. We do not claim the architecture as a contribution. The differences lie in what the abstention decision conditions on (variables intended to proxy adverse-selection exposure, with functional-form restrictions derived from an explicit model), in the risk quantity for which a guarantee is sought under temporal dependence, and in the market setting.

## 2. Selective classification and distribution-free risk control

The reject option goes back to Chow [`chow1970optimum`]〔仅题录〕. Modern selective classification studies the trade-off between coverage and risk on the accepted set; Geifman and El-Yaniv [`geifman2017selective`]〔仅读摘要〕 give a procedure that selects a confidence threshold with a high-probability bound on selective risk, and SelectiveNet [`geifman2019selectivenet`]〔仅读摘要〕 trains predictor and selector jointly. Cortes, DeSalvo and Mohri [`cortes2016learning`]〔仅题录〕 develop a learning theory for rejection. Deep Gamblers [`ziyin2019deepgamblers`] recasts m-class selective classification as an (m+1)-class problem whose loss is the doubling rate of a horse-race gamble, so that abstaining corresponds to reserving part of one's stake; its theorems characterize the optimal betting solution rather than bound selective risk, and its experiments are on image benchmarks. Zhang et al. [`zhang2023abstain`], motivated explicitly by finance, posit a generative process in which a large share of samples is uninformative, propose a selector loss, and prove minimax-optimal sample complexity for recovering the informative region; the analysis assumes i.i.d. samples and the experiments contain no trading evaluation.

A parallel literature calibrates a threshold after training so that a user-specified risk is controlled without distributional assumptions: risk-controlling prediction sets [`bates2021rcps`]〔仅读摘要〕, Learn-then-Test [`angelopoulos2021ltt`]〔仅读摘要〕 and conformal risk control [`angelopoulos2022crc`]〔仅读摘要〕. Several recent works combine this with selection, controlling risk on the accepted cases under exchangeability [`xu2025scrc`, `bai2026score`]〔均仅读摘要〕.

Exchangeability is implausible for financial return streams. Adaptive conformal inference [`gibbs2021aci`]〔仅读摘要〕 achieves the target long-run coverage frequency for arbitrary data-generating processes. Time-uniform confidence sequences [`howard2021timeuniform`]〔仅读摘要〕 and the broader framework of game-theoretic, anytime-valid inference [`ramdas2023savi`]〔仅读摘要〕 provide guarantees that hold at arbitrary stopping times. Anytime-valid constructions based on betting martingales extend risk control to sequential, adaptively collected data [`xu2024arcps`, `hultberg2026anytime`]〔均仅读摘要〕, and Khosravi and Huo [`khosravi2026csa`]〔仅读摘要〕 maintain one e-process per threshold to obtain an anytime, pathwise bound on *selective* risk under predictable updates, in the context of deploying language models. Online conformal risk control has been extended to delayed and partially lost feedback [`wang2026caocrc`]〔仅读摘要〕, online selective classification has been analysed against adaptive adversaries [`gangrade2021online`]〔仅读摘要〕, and selective classification under distribution shift has been studied empirically [`liang2024selective`]〔仅读摘要〕.

These results supply the statistical machinery we build on. To our knowledge they have not been instantiated for the risk "fraction of executed trades that lose money" on a trading stream, where labels mature only after the holding period, positions overlap, and same-day decisions across stocks are strongly dependent; our theoretical contribution should be read as an adaptation of anytime-valid selective risk control to that setting, not as a new type of guarantee.

## 3. Uncertainty-aware and safe portfolio construction

A neighbouring literature attaches guarantees to portfolio-level decisions rather than to individual trades. MohammadpourAzari, Zarei and Fazli [`mohammadpourazari2026safe`] let a controller choose among a prediction-independent Safe portfolio, a Follow overlay and a Reverse overlay; an evidence gate transfers unsupported directional mass to Safe, which is their notion of abstention. They prove tracking regret against switching comparators under delayed feedback and a pathwise collateral safety bound, both for arbitrary bounded reward sequences, and report that on a frozen holdout of 82 U.S. stocks neither signal orientation outperforms equal weighting. Zarei, MohammadpourAzari and Habibi [`zarei2026conformal`] gate the proposals of a reinforcement-learning allocator on nine U.S. sector ETFs with a conformally calibrated, text-conditioned risk budget; the calibration relies on approximate local exchangeability within rolling buckets. Conformal methods have also been used to form portfolios from prediction intervals [`kato2024conformal`]〔仅读摘要〕, to wrap stock-selection classifiers [`kaya2025conformal`]〔仅读摘要〕, to size positions [`ryan2026conformalkelly`]〔仅读摘要〕, and to calibrate portfolio tail-risk measures under non-stationarity [`schmitt2026taming`, `chen2026cvar`]〔均仅读摘要〕. Ryan's pre-registered study is a useful caution: marginal coverage transferred to the sealed test window while the economic performance did not. Benhamou et al. [`benhamou2026promote`]〔仅读摘要〕 use e-processes to control the probability of ever promoting a trading rule with non-positive expected net score, showing that anytime-valid inference has reached trading research, though at the level of rules rather than individual trades.

Relative to this literature, the object we seek to control is different: the loss probability of selected trades rather than relative wealth, regret, or a portfolio tail quantile. We deliberately do not claim guarantees for drawdown or portfolio CVaR, which depend on sizing and on the dependence between overlapping positions.

> 中文备注

> - 与本研究的关系：MohammadpourAzari et al. 已处理"延迟反馈 + 重叠持仓 + 弃权 + 无分布假设"，只是目标量不同（财富 vs 亏损概率）。
> - 可作为基线吗：证据门控可抽出来作基线；完整系统复现成本高，建议只在相关工作中对比保证类型。RL 类基线（FinRL [`liu2020finrl`]〔仅读摘要〕、集成策略 [`yang2020ensemble`]〔仅题录〕）v2 已降为可选。

## 4. Strategic trading, adverse selection and the decision not to trade

Economic theory has long formalized why an uninformed party should hesitate to trade. The no-trade theorem of Milgrom and Stokey [`milgrom1982information`]〔仅题录〕 rules out purely informational trade among rational agents with common priors; Kyle [`kyle1985continuous`]〔仅题录〕 and Glosten and Milgrom [`glosten1985bid`]〔仅题录〕 model informed traders facing uninformed counterparties, with adverse selection as the source of price impact and spreads; Rock [`rock1986new`]〔仅题录〕 analyses the winner's curse faced by uninformed investors in new issues; and Dow and Werlang [`dow1992uncertainty`]〔仅题录〕 and Easley and O'Hara [`easley2009ambiguity`]〔仅题录〕 study non-participation under ambiguity. Empirical proxies for informed trading such as PIN [`easley1996liquidity`]〔仅题录〕 and VPIN [`easley2012flow`]〔仅题录〕 require order-flow data. Whether information asymmetry is a cost to be avoided or a priced risk is itself contested: Easley, Hvidkjaer and O'Hara [`easley2002information`]〔仅读摘要〕 estimate the probability of information-based trading for NYSE stocks over 1983–1998 and report that a difference of 10 percentage points in that probability corresponds to a difference of 2.5 percent per year in expected returns, and Choi, Jin and Yan [`choi2013informed`]〔NBER 版正文已读，表格未逐一核对；期刊版 `choi2025informed` 未读〕, using account-level holdings data from the Shanghai Stock Exchange for 1996–2007, report that stocks in the top quintile of prior institutional ownership volatility, their predictor of institutional information advantage, earn a four-factor alpha 0.90% per month higher than the bottom quintile; the raw return difference is not statistically significant and the effect is absent among small stocks.

Closest in spirit to the poker analogy, Chakraborty and Yilmaz [`chakraborty2008bluffing`]〔正文通读；在线附录的引理证明未读〕 study a two-period model with an insider, a continuum of rational followers who know whether informed trading is present but not the asset's value, and competitive market makers; they show that the insider bluffs in every equilibrium, trading against his information early in order to trade profitably later, because followers who mimicked first-period order flow would otherwise compete away his profits. The result concerns the informed party; the paper does not characterize when the less informed should refrain from trading. Game-theoretic treatments of trading in the recent quantitative literature mostly concern execution and market making: position building as a game of incomplete information [`chriss2025position`]〔仅读摘要〕, adverse selection of meta-orders by learning market makers [`jafree2025adverse`]〔仅读摘要〕, and brokers who predict whether client flow is toxic and decide whether to internalize it [`cartea2023toxic`, `cartea2025simple`]〔均仅读摘要；前者已发表于 Quantitative Finance，期刊版 key 为 `cartea2026toxic`，二者只引其一〕. The last is a learned adverse-selection filter, but for a liquidity provider with proprietary trade data rather than for a directional investor with public daily data. Li and Deng [`li2025willai`]〔仅读摘要〕 revisit the no-trade theorem for computationally bounded agents, and Benhamou et al. [`benhamou2026collision`]〔仅读摘要〕 derive a penalty on common exposures from a linear-impact game among allocators. Superhuman poker agents [`brown2018libratus`, `brown2019pluribus`]〔均仅题录〕 demonstrate that equilibrium-approximating play under imperfect information is computable, but no work we found transfers the fold decision to equity entry rules.

We use this literature in two ways: as the source of a minimal informed-trader model from which an entry threshold can be derived, and as a warning that the sign of the relation between adverse-selection proxies and subsequent returns must be established empirically rather than assumed.

## 5. Cost-basis and turnover variables; machine learning in the Chinese stock market

The "chip distribution" used by Chinese retail trading software estimates the distribution of holders' purchase prices from the history of prices and turnover. Its academic counterpart is the capital gains overhang of Grinblatt and Han [`grinblatt2005prospect`]〔仅题录〕, a turnover-weighted reference price motivated by the disposition effect, later used by Wang, Yan and Yu [`wang2017reference`]〔仅题录〕 to study the risk–return trade-off. Evidence for China differs from the U.S.: Xu [`xu2025replication`]〔仅读摘要〕 replicates Wang et al. on 1995–2024 data and finds that the interaction between capital gains overhang and risk proxies is predominantly negative in the Chinese market, and Pan, Fan and Zhang [`pan2025overreaction`]〔仅读摘要；期刊质量未评估〕 report a negative cross-sectional relation between capital gains and returns for A-shares. A conference paper proposes a stock-picking strategy from chip-distribution indicators [`zhang2020chip`]〔仅题录〕. Abnormal turnover enters the Chinese factor model of Liu, Stambaugh and Yuan [`liu2019size`]〔仅题录〕, and crowding in the associated factor has been examined [`xing2024pmo`]〔仅题录〕. In this literature these variables are behavioural return predictors; we are not aware of work that evaluates their incremental value for an abstention decision at matched coverage.

Machine-learning return prediction for A-shares is benchmarked by Leippold, Wang and Zhou [`leippold2022machine`]〔仅题录〕, following Gu, Kelly and Xiu [`gu2020empirical`]〔仅题录〕; factor timing in China is studied in [`ma2023timing`, `wu2024factor`]〔均仅题录〕. Market-specific trading rules have received some attention: Zhang et al. [`zhang2022t1`]〔仅读摘要〕 incorporate the T+1 restriction into the reward of a reinforcement-learning trading framework, Liang and Hu [`liang2024pricelimit`]〔仅读摘要〕 predict price-limit hits, and Liu et al. [`liu2026astockarena`]〔仅读摘要〕 audit LLM trading agents on A-shares. We did not find a study of selective trading on individual A-shares that models T+1 and price-limit non-executability explicitly.
