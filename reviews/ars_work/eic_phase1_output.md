## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From the editorial-oversight seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design, its handling of data, the way statistical evidence is reported, and the provisions made for others to reproduce the work reach the standard that peer reviewers in the field would demand. It is the mandatory dimension of this contract and is owned and scored by the methodology seat, not by me; I will not plan or issue a score for it. My oversight obligation is to understand that any warn or block on it drives the outcome to major revision, and that a fatal block on it drives the outcome to rejection, so nothing I write under my own dimension should be read as softening or substituting for that judgment.

D2 (writing_and_structure): This dimension asks whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can follow it, whether the exposition is clear, and whether it respects the conventions that method-focused papers are expected to follow at their intended outlet. It is a normal-priority dimension that I own and am the only eligible role to score. A warn or worse here moves the outcome to minor revision at most on its own; it cannot by itself produce a harsher outcome, and a pass here contributes to acceptance only if every other dimension also passes.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated question, the planned analyses, and the conclusions each can support are laid out in an order a reader can follow without back-tracking; whether terms and notation are defined before use and used consistently; whether the length is justified by content rather than repetition; whether the wording of claims in the title, abstract, and conclusions is kept in proportion to the scope of evidence the document itself says it has; and whether the document follows the structural conventions expected of a method-focused paper of its declared type.
what_triggers_block: The organisation or exposition is deficient enough that a qualified reader cannot determine what is being claimed, what would count as support or refutation, or how the parts relate, or the framing statements in title, abstract, or conclusions assert materially more than the body is structured to support, so that the document would need restructuring rather than editing.
what_triggers_warn: The document is followable and its claims are identifiable, but there are correctable expository problems such as redundant or excessive length, sections out of logical order, inconsistent terminology or notation, missing signposting, departures from expected conventions for method-focused papers, or isolated phrases whose strength exceeds the stated scope and can be fixed by rewording.
