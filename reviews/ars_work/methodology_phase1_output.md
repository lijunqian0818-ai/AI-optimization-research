## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): As the methodology seat I own this mandatory dimension. I read it as asking whether the study's design, its handling of data, its statistical reporting, and the means it offers for reproduction would survive scrutiny by a specialist referee in the field named in the metadata. From a rigor standpoint that means: every claim must rest on stated assumptions and conventions that are sufficient for it; every confirmatory test must have a defined estimand, comparator, null hypothesis and decision rule fixed before outcomes are seen; inference must respect the dependence structure of the data and the number of comparisons made; information available at decision time must be separated from information that arrives later; competing methods must be treated even-handedly; and a third party must be able to rerun the analysis from the described materials. Because the dimension is mandatory, a defect that invalidates the central inferential logic and cannot be repaired by revision is fatal, a defect that leaves a central claim unsupported but repairable is a block, and a defect that weakens secondary claims or leaves reporting incomplete is a warn.

D2 (writing_and_structure): This normal-priority dimension concerns how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it explains itself, and whether it follows the conventions expected of a method-focused paper. It is owned by, and only eligible for, the editor-in-chief seat, so I will not plan or assign a score for it. From a methodology perspective I understand its relevance only as follows: exposition matters to me to the extent that unclear or scattered description prevents a reader from determining what was actually done, and where that happens I will record the consequence under D1 as a rigor or reproducibility problem rather than as a writing judgement.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Whether each claim is backed by explicit and sufficient assumptions and accounting or execution conventions; whether each confirmatory test states its estimand, comparator, null, decision rule and power or sensitivity analysis in advance; whether equivalence is tested as equivalence rather than inferred from a non-significant difference; whether the reported metric matches the inference target; whether multiplicity is controlled and inference is robust to serial and cross-sectional dependence; whether data are point-in-time with no look-ahead, the deployed procedure is frozen before evaluation, hold-out data are untouched, tuning budgets are equal across methods, and variables defined only on a subsample are handled without selection distortion; whether decompositions reconcile exactly with the underlying records; whether a full-pipeline null simulation or equivalent calibration check exists; whether code, seeds, data provenance and deviation rules allow reproduction; and whether issues raised in earlier review rounds are verifiably closed rather than reworded.
what_triggers_block: A repairable defect that leaves at least one central claim without valid support, such as a confirmatory test whose estimand, comparator or null is undefined or inconsistent across sections, a headline metric that differs from the quantity the inference targets, a decomposition that does not reconcile with its source records, absent or inadequate multiplicity or dependence handling for a primary test, a plausible but unquantified leakage channel, unequal tuning budgets across compared methods, or a previously raised critical issue that remains unresolved.
what_triggers_warn: Central inferential logic is sound but secondary matters are incomplete, such as missing or weakly justified power or sensitivity analysis for non-primary tests, robustness checks that are named but not specified, minor ambiguities in conventions that do not change a primary conclusion, exploratory analyses insufficiently distinguished from confirmatory ones, or reproducibility materials that are described but partly missing.
what_triggers_fatal: A defect that invalidates the central inferential logic and cannot be fixed by revision within the stated design, such as demonstrated use of future or hold-out information in constructing or selecting the evaluated procedure, primary hypotheses or decision rules evidently set after outcomes were observed while presented as pre-specified, a primary estimand that is not identifiable from the described data under any stated assumption, or fabricated, irreproducible-by-construction or internally contradictory primary evidence.
